Sec. 01

Method of use

01

Save your plate

Add your vehicles once. Keep several plates and switch which one is selected in a tap.

02

Pick your zone

Extra, Red, Blue and White zones, plus daily tickets and seasonal locations, each with its SMS number and time limit.

03

Send the SMS

The app opens your messaging app with the plate and zone already filled in. Payment goes through your mobile operator — no card, no sign-up.
